# Descriptions, alt text and boards

## Description structure (up to 500 characters)

1. **Hook sentence with the primary keyword** (first ~50 characters show in many views).
2. **One or two sentences of specifics**: what's inside, who it's for, what makes it different.
   Work in 2-4 secondary keywords naturally.
3. **Soft call to action**: "Save this for later", "Get the full recipe on the blog", "Tap to see all 17 ideas".

Write for a person first. Pinterest understands natural language; keyword lists and repetition look spammy.

### Examples

Every detail in these examples is assumed to come from the page; don't copy details the page doesn't state.

Recipe:
> Easy chicken dinner recipes for busy weeknights, all ready in 30 minutes or less. These 17
> family-friendly meals use simple pantry ingredients, from one-pan lemon chicken to creamy Tuscan
> skillets. Save this for your next meal plan and get every recipe on the blog.

Home:
> Small bathroom ideas that make a tiny space feel bigger. See 25 budget-friendly updates, from
> floating shelves and light paint colors to smart storage over the toilet. Save it for your next
> bathroom makeover.

Product:
> A minimalist leather wallet that holds 8 cards and still fits a front pocket. Slim, RFID-blocking
> and handmade from full-grain leather. Shop the wallet on our site.

## Alt text

- Describe what's actually in the image, plainly: subject, setting, notable details, and the
  headline text on the image.
- 1-2 sentences. Include the primary keyword only if it fits naturally.
- Example: "Overhead view of a one-pan lemon chicken dinner with roasted potatoes and green beans, with
  the text '17 Easy Chicken Dinners'."

## Boards

- Name boards with the keyword people search: "Easy Chicken Dinner Recipes", "Small Bathroom Ideas".
- Suggest the most specific relevant board; a focused board beats a catch-all.
- Board descriptions: one or two sentences with the main keywords.

## Hashtags

Optional and of limited value today. If the user wants them, 2-3 specific ones at the end of the
description. Never a block of 20.

## Fresh pins and timing

- Pinterest favors fresh images. New pins for the same page with a new photo, headline or template
  count as fresh; re-pinning the identical image does not.
- Space out pins for the same URL (days or weeks apart) rather than posting many at once.
- Seasonal content: people plan ahead, so publish seasonal pins 1-3 months before the season or holiday.

## Honesty

- The pin, title and description must match what the linked page delivers.
- No misleading health, financial or "too good to be true" claims.
- Disclose affiliate links or sponsored content as the user's local rules require (for example "#ad" or "affiliate link").
