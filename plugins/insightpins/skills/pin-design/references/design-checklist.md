# Pin design checklist

Use for checking a rendered preview, reviewing an uploaded pin, or planning a redesign.
Each item is a yes/no question. A "no" on any item marked **(critical)** is worth fixing before posting.

## Format

- [ ] **(critical)** Vertical, about 2:3 (1000x1500). Not square, horizontal or extremely tall.
- [ ] Image is sharp at full size; no visible compression, blur or stretching.

## Readability at thumbnail size (imagine it ~230 px wide)

- [ ] **(critical)** Headline readable at a glance.
- [ ] Headline is short: ideally 3-8 words, at most about 3 lines.
- [ ] **(critical)** Strong contrast between the headline and its background.
- [ ] **(critical)** Subtitle and site name also readable (a 4.5:1 contrast ratio or more; bold
      button labels at least 3:1); they are small and often drawn lighter than the headline. White small text on a
      mid-tone panel (coral, orange, bright blue, lime) fails.
- [ ] Heavy enough type weight; thin script fonts used only for small accents.
- [ ] Subtitle, if any, is short (one line or two) and clearly secondary.

## Layout

- [ ] One focal point (one main photo subject, one headline).
- [ ] Clear hierarchy: headline > subtitle > CTA/site name.
- [ ] Text sits on a calm area or a solid panel; not over faces, food or busy detail.
- [ ] Nothing important touching the edges or in the bottom-right corner.
- [ ] Two fonts at most; consistent alignment.

## Photo

- [ ] **(critical)** Relevant to the headline and to what the page actually delivers.
- [ ] Shows the result or benefit (finished dish, styled room, the destination, the product in use).
- [ ] Bright, well lit, natural colors.
- [ ] Subject well cropped (no cut-off heads or products).
- [ ] No text, watermark or logo baked into the photo (other than a product's own label).
- [ ] The user has the right to publish it (own photo, licensed stock, or permission).

## Color

- [ ] Text panels and badges stand apart from the photo by lightness (dark next to bright, light
      next to dark), not only by hue.
- [ ] Colors fit the topic's mood (red, coral and orange for food, deals and fun; cooler or
      neutral tones for calm, winter, wellness and luxury topics).
- [ ] The palette doesn't fight the photo (a color that echoes one in the photo looks designed).
- [ ] Consistent with the brand's other pins, if known.

## Message

- [ ] **(critical)** Clear, specific promise: the viewer knows what they get by clicking.
- [ ] Uses a strong angle: a number, an outcome, a time or money saving, a problem solved, an audience ("for small spaces").
- [ ] No clickbait or claims the page doesn't back up.
- [ ] CTA fits the content ("Get the Recipe", "Shop Now", "Read the Guide").

## Branding

- [ ] Site name or domain visible but small.
- [ ] Looks like the brand's other pins (palette, fonts), without looking like an ad.

## Pinterest fit

- [ ] Feels like inspiration or useful information, not a banner ad.
- [ ] Topic and photo would make sense in a search result for the main keyword.
