# Pin scoring rubric

Score each criterion 1-10. Overall = weighted average, rounded to one decimal.

| # | Criterion | Weight | What a 9-10 looks like | What a 1-4 looks like |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| 1 | Thumbnail readability | 20% | Headline reads instantly at ~230 px wide; big, heavy, high-contrast type | Text tiny, thin, low-contrast or over a busy photo |
| 2 | Clarity of promise | 20% | Viewer knows exactly what they get by clicking; specific and concrete | Vague, clever-only or no headline; unclear what the pin is about |
| 3 | Photo quality and relevance | 15% | Sharp, bright, shows the result/benefit, matches the headline | Blurry, dark, generic, unrelated, watermarked |
| 4 | Layout and hierarchy | 15% | One focal point; clear headline > subtitle > CTA; text on a calm area | Cluttered, competing elements, no clear reading order |
| 5 | Scroll-stopping power | 10% | Stands out in a feed: contrast, a strong number or angle, distinct look | Blends in; looks like every other pin in the niche |
| 6 | Format | 10% | Vertical ~2:3, high resolution, safe edges respected | Square/horizontal, too tall (cut off), pixelated, text at edges |
| 7 | Branding | 5% | Small site name/brand mark; consistent style | No brand at all, or a large logo that makes it look like an ad |
| 8 | Trust and honesty | 5% | Pin matches what the page delivers; no exaggerated claims | Clickbait, misleading image, unsupported health/money claims |

**Text-only pins** (quote cards, typography pins): score criterion 3 on the visual treatment instead
of a photo: does the background, color and type styling look intentional and eye-catching, or plain
and flat? A plain pale gradient with thin text is a 4-5.

Weighted overall = 0.20*(1) + 0.20*(2) + 0.15*(3) + 0.15*(4) + 0.10*(5) + 0.10*(6) + 0.05*(7) + 0.05*(8)

## Score guide

- **9-10**: ready to post; at most minor polish.
- **7-8**: good; one or two clear improvements.
- **5-6**: average; several fixes would noticeably improve clicks.
- **3-4**: weak; needs a redesign of the headline, photo or layout.
- **1-2**: not usable as a pin (wrong format, unreadable, no message).

## If copy is provided

Also check, without adding to the score:
- Pin title: primary keyword near the start, clear benefit, under 100 characters.
- Description: keyword in the first sentence, natural language, specifics, soft CTA, under 500 characters.
- Alt text present and descriptive.
- Copy matches the image and the page.
