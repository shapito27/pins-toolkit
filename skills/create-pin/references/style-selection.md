# Palette and font selection

Always call `list_styles` first; palettes and font pairings can change. Use this guide to choose.

## Rules that matter more than taste

1. **Contrast with the photo.** The palette colors fill bands, badges and text. Pick colors that
   stand apart from the photo's dominant colors: a blue palette on a beach photo disappears, a warm
   palette pops. Green food photo -> warm or berry tones; warm sunset photo -> blues or neutrals.
2. **Readable text.** Text color against its background must be high contrast. Light-on-light or
   mid-tone-on-mid-tone fails at thumbnail size.
3. **Brand consistency.** If the user has a brand color or used a palette on earlier pins, reuse it.
   Recognizable pins across a feed build trust. Ask once if you don't know; otherwise follow the guide below.
4. **One mood per pin.** Font pairing and palette should say the same thing (elegant, playful, bold, calm).

## Palette by mood and topic

| Mood / topic | Palette families to try |
| - | - |
| Food, cooking, baking, autumn | Warm Tones (terracotta, sunset-glow), Earth & Neutral (warm-earth) |
| Home decor, interiors, wedding, beauty | Pink & Rose (rose-gold, dusty-rose), Earth & Neutral |
| Health, wellness, gardening, nature, sustainability | Greens & Teals (sage, forest-calm, cool-mint) |
| Travel, summer, water, outdoors | Blues & Purples (ocean-breeze), Greens & Teals (electric) |
| Finance, business, career, tech, productivity | Blues & Purples (midnight), Earth & Neutral (minimalist) |
| Kids, crafts, parties, fun | Warm Tones (coral-reef), Pink & Rose (berry-blush), Greens & Teals (electric) |
| Fashion, luxury, minimal aesthetic | Earth & Neutral (minimalist), Pink & Rose (rose-gold) |
| Spirituality, self-care, quotes | Blues & Purples (lavender), Pink & Rose (dusty-rose) |
| Sales, deals, urgent or bold | Warm Tones (coral-reef, sunset-glow) |

These are starting points. Rule 1 (contrast with the photo) wins over the table.

## Font pairing by mood

| Mood | Font pairings |
| - | - |
| Bold, attention-grabbing, listicles, fitness, deals | bold-impact, modern-sans |
| Clean, modern, how-to, business, tech | modern-sans, minimalist, tech-modern, professional |
| Elegant, home, wedding, beauty, luxury | classic-serif, editorial |
| Warm, family, food, lifestyle | friendly, classic-serif |
| Fun, kids, casual | playful, friendly |
| Artistic, fashion, creative | creative, editorial |

Thin or decorative display fonts are harder to read at thumbnail size; with them, raise
`title_size` and keep the headline short.
